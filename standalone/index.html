Road Safety & Help

Highway Safety Protocols & FAQs

Essential emergency precautions to protect you and your passengers before assistance arrives.

⚠️ What should I do immediately when my vehicle breaks down on the highway? ▼
Turn on hazard warning lights immediately. Gently steer vehicle to extreme left shoulder. Exit all passengers from left passenger side doors and stand safely behind the highway crash barrier. Never remain seated inside a stationary car on high-speed expressways.
🚨 Can I use Highway SOS without creating an account or logging in? ▼
Yes! Help on Drive features a dedicated Highway Emergency Guest SOS mode. Anyone can click the red SOS HELP button to instantly copy GPS coordinates and direct-dial Police (112), Ambulance (108), or NHAI Expressway Control (1033).
📐 Where should I place the reflective hazard triangle? ▼
On high-speed highways and expressways, place your red reflective warning triangle at least 45 to 50 meters (approximately 60 steps) behind your disabled vehicle in the same lane/shoulder to give high-speed vehicles sufficient braking reaction distance.
🧾 How are service charges and GST invoices handled? ▼
Pricing is 100% transparent and calculated upfront (Base service charge + ₹25/km transit + ₹50 platform fee + 18% GST). Upon service completion, an official printable GST tax invoice is generated automatically. Settle conveniently via UPI, Card, or Cash.
👨‍✈️ Are drivers and mechanics background verified? ▼
Yes. Every mechanic and chauffeur undergoes strict document verification including Commercial Driving License verification, Aadhaar KYC, Police background checks, and minimum 4+ years of professional driving experience.
