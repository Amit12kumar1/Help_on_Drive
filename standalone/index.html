Highway Protocols

Road Safety & Important Guidelines

What to do immediately when stranded on an expressway before help arrives.

⚠️ 1. Pull Over to the Hard Shoulder

Turn on hazard warning lights immediately. Gently guide the vehicle to the extreme left lane or service shoulder away from fast highway traffic.

📐 2. Place Warning Triangle

Place your reflective emergency triangle at least 45 to 50 meters behind your vehicle so approaching vehicles have sufficient braking distance.

🛡️ 3. Stand Behind Highway Crash Barriers

Never remain seated inside a stranded car on high-speed expressways. Exit from the left passenger side and stand safely behind the steel crash barrier.

🚨 4. Use 1-Click Highway SOS

Click the red SOS HELP button on this platform. It works without login, copies exact GPS coordinates, and provides direct dial to 112 / 1033.
