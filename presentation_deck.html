2. Motivation & Gap Analysis

The Real-World Mobility Crisis

Current Informal Breakdown Problems
  • No Guaranteed Arrival ETA: Drivers stranded on highways wait 2 to 4 hours with no visibility into mechanic location.
  • Extortionate Roadside Surge Pricing: Unregulated local mechanics demand arbitrary charges (₹2000+ for minor punctures).
  • Safety Risks on Expressways: 70% of highway rear-end fatalities occur when vehicles are stationary in breakdown condition.
  • Basement & Low-Clearance Failures: Standard tow trucks cannot enter tight multi-level parking basements when batteries die.
Current Driver Hiring Dilemmas
  • Unverified Driver Safety: Relying on casual neighborhood referrals with zero commercial licence verification or criminal background checks.
  • Rigid Duration Tiers: Existing cab aggregators provide their own cars at exorbitant rates, with no platform to drive the owner's personal car.
  • No Digital Paper Trail: Lack of GST tax invoices, formal trip logs, and transparent per-hour billing.
  • Zero Panic Response: No connected panic mechanism during late-night outstation commutes.
Solution Requirement: A single digital umbrella providing verified rapid assistance, personal chauffeurs, and emergency telematics.
3. System Objectives

Project Scope & Technical Goals

Sub-15 Minute Dispatch SLA

Automate geographical matching of vehicle distress beacons with the nearest certified mobile technician or towing carrier within 3-5 km radius.

3-Tier Vetting & Verification Pipeline

Enforce mandatory commercial driver license verification, Aadhaar identity check, and workshop registration vetting before accepting orders.

Real-Time Bi-Directional Telematics

Implement WebSockets (Socket.IO) and Leaflet GPS mapping to stream live technician/driver coordinates with sub-second latency and accurate ETA.

1-Tap Emergency SOS & Panic Dispatch

Instantaneous hardware siren trigger, precise GPS coordinate broadcast to registered family contacts, and 1-tap connection to 112/108 services.

Audience: Daily commuters, long-distance highway travelers, elderly drivers, corporate executives, and commercial fleet owners.

9. Core Innovation #3

1-Click SOS Panic & MoRTH Road Safety

Emergency SOS Panic Protocol
  • Audio Siren Alarm: Uses Web Audio API oscillator synthesis to emit an urgent audible siren upon panic trigger.
  • Precision GPS Locking: Captures exact latitude/longitude with timestamp and generates a live tracking URL.
  • Emergency Contact Broadcast: Automatically alerts configured family members (Father, Mother, Friend) with distress SMS payload.
  • National Highway Hotline Sync: Direct 1-tap links to 112 (Police), 108 (Ambulance), and 1033 (NHAI Road Helpline).
MoRTH 5 Golden Rules of Road Safety
  • Rule 1: Never Sit in Stalled Car: Always exit left and wait behind the steel crash barrier on expressways.
  • Rule 2: 50m Warning Triangle: Place reflective triangle 50-100m behind vehicle to give 100km/h traffic braking buffer.
  • Rule 3: Tyre Blowout Handling: No sudden braking; hold steering straight and let engine braking decelerate vehicle.
  • Rule 4: High-Beam Discipline: Mandatory low-beam dip during highway traffic encounters to prevent blinding accidents.
  • Rule 5: Zero Drowsy Driving: Fatigue countermeasures with 24/7 on-demand night chauffeurs.
Dedicated knowledge base implemented at /faq tab under Road Safety & Rules.
10. Technical Architecture

Real-Time Socket.IO Telematics Pipeline

1. Broadcast Dispatch

When customer submits breakdown request, server emits assistance:new event to mechanics in nearby spatial radius.

2. Live GPS Coordinate Stream

Technician/Driver device continuously streams geo-coordinates via driver:location_update with automatic Leaflet marker interpolation.

3. State Transition Machine

Strict lifecycle progression: pending → accepted → on_the_way → arrived → in_progress → completed.

In-App Web Chat & Coordination:

Bi-directional customer-technician chat room scoped to booking ID (chat:message), enabling instantaneous landmark clarification, photo sharing, and arrival confirmation without revealing personal contact numbers.

Enterprise-Grade Security Architecture
  • Stateful JWT Session Guards: JSON Web Tokens signed with 256-bit secrets, expiring in 30 days.
  • Bcrypt Salt Hashing: Passwords hashed with 10 salt rounds; plain-text passwords never stored.
  • Zero Sensitive Leakage: All database schemas explicitly exclude password on API query returns.
  • Input Sanitization: Protected against NoSQL injection and XSS payload injections.
